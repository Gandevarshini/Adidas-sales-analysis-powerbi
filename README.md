# Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard
## 📊 Project Overview
The **Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive **Power BI Data Analytics project** developed to analyze Adidas sales performance and generate meaningful business insights.
The dashboard provides a clear view of sales, profit, units sold, average price, profit margin, monthly sales trends, state-wise sales, regional performance, product-wise sales, and retailer-wise sales.
## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze overall Adidas sales performance
- Monitor total sales and profit
- Analyze monthly sales trends
- Compare sales across different regions and states
- Analyze product-wise sales performance
- Compare retailer-wise sales
- Track average price and profit margin
- Identify important sales patterns and trends
## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI**
- **Power Query**
- **DAX**
- **Microsoft Excel / CSV**
- **Data Visualization**

## 📈 Dashboard Features
### Monthly Sales Trend
Analyzes Adidas sales performance from January to December to identify monthly trends and changes.
### State-wise Sales
Uses a map visualization to analyze sales performance across different states.
### Region-wise Sales
Provides sales analysis across:
- West
- Northeast
- Southeast
- South
- Midwest
- East
### Product-wise Sales
Analyzes sales across different Adidas product categories, including:
- Men's Street Footwear
- Women's Apparel
- Men's Athletic Footwear
- Women's Street Footwear
- Men's Apparel
- Women's Athletic Footwear
### Retailer-wise Sales
Compares sales performance across major retailers such as:
- West Gear
- Foot Locker
- Sports Direct
- Kohl's
- Amazon
- Walmart
